Site Visit

Geary Rapid Project

Improving transit and safety along San Francisco’s busiest bus corridor

Take a bus and walking tour along Geary Boulevard to view transit and safety improvements completed in 2021 through the San Francisco Municipal Transportation Agency’s (SFMTA’s) Geary Rapid Project. Learn how the project:

  • Re-connected neighborhoods divided during San Francisco’s urban renewal era;
  • Improved traffic safety and reduced excessive speeding through changes like a road diet, new signalized pedestrian crossings, pedestrian bulb-outs, and median refuges;
  • Improved travel time and reliability for the >47,000 daily Muni 38/38R Geary riders through new dedicated red transit lanes, optimized bus stops, transit signal priority, and bus bulbs.

This tour will begin at Geary Boulevard and Van Ness Avenue, the intersection of two of San Francisco’s premier transit corridors (meet at the northwest corner). The group will then take a brief ride on the 38/38R Geary, alight at Laguna Street and then walk about one mile through the heart of the project area where the Western Addition, Japantown and Fillmore District meet. Please dress for the weather, wear comfortable walking shoes, and be prepared to pay Muni fare (Clipper or tap to pay).

Site Visit

Balboa Park Station: Transit Improvements and Transit-Oriented Development

The tour will highlight how BART and the City of San Francisco are collaborating to address circulation, access, and land use challenges, improve connections to the station and between transit modes, and support the City’s vision for bringing more housing to the neighborhood. Recognizing Balboa Park Station’s role as a major intermodal transit hub in southern San Francisco and its proximity to City College, BART has invested in station modernization while working with the City to create a safer, more accessible, better-connected, and vibrant neighborhood.
