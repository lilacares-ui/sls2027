Mailer Highlights

Rough Conference Program

Three Days, Start to Finish

A rough outline of the weekend. The full itinerary will be posted here as it is finalized.

Day 1

Begins 1 PM

  • Site Visits across the bay, led by various professionals.
  • Berkeley Scavenger Hunt for a more adventurous introduction to the city hosting this year’s summit, at the same time as the Site Visits.
  • Evening Mixer for a relaxed way to reconnect, socialize, and grow your ITE Western District community, and to get you excited for the next day of the summit.
Day 2

Full day

  • Group Photo and Icebreakers to kick off the day.
  • Professional Presentations to get up to date on the latest transportation trends, tech, and projects shaping how we move.
  • Workshops that give you hands-on experience to build the skills employers are looking for.
  • Resume Workshop & Speed Interviews as practice for the Career Fair, all to give you the best shot at your goals (employment, research, grad school, etc.).
  • Student Presentations to show off your projects, sharpen your speaking skills, and lead the conversation.
  • Roundtable Discussions to spark meaningful conversations about the current state of transportation, policy, AI, and more.
  • Professional Headshots to update LinkedIn.
  • The Banquet to round out the night.
Day 3

Ends 3 PM

  • Traffic Bowl to keep your industry knowledge sharp.
  • More site visits to different places for those who can’t make it on Day 1, and for those who want more opportunities to see and connect in the bay.

Call for Student Presentations

SLS 2027 Student Presentations

For SLS 2027, we are excited to introduce student presentations. This is an opportunity for students across the Western District to lead the conversation, whether that is from their research, internship knowledge, or lived experiences.

The Following Topics Are Welcome

  • Academic and creative work
  • Leadership and personal development
  • Career and professional readiness
  • Applied projects and service
  • Skills and workshops
  • + More!

Who Can Apply

Any student attending SLS can apply.
Applications are due November 15th, 11:59 PM PST.

Please noteWe hope to give everyone the opportunity to present. However, if demand exceeds the capacity we can provide, applications will be subject to a rubric and may be denied.
