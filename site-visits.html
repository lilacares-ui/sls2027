Site Visit

Balboa Park Station: Transit Improvements and Transit-Oriented Development

The tour will highlight how BART and the City of San Francisco are collaborating to address circulation, access, and land use challenges, improve connections to the station and between transit modes, and support the City’s vision for bringing more housing to the neighborhood. Recognizing Balboa Park Station’s role as a major intermodal transit hub in southern San Francisco and its proximity to City College, BART has invested in station modernization while working with the City to create a safer, more accessible, better-connected, and vibrant neighborhood.
